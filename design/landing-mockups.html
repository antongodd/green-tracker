A landing for every new product

You asked for something special when a new product goes in, like a rocket landing into the Leaderboard, different for each band, with 9.5 the biggest show. Pick a way, pick a score, then tap Save on the phone (or Play below). Made-up products only.

1. The way
2. Its score
3. Colour set

What grows band by band

  • Under 8.0: it lands with a little puff of dust. Every product gets a landing.
  • 8.0 to 8.4: the dust, then a glint of its colour across the row.
  • 8.5 to 8.9: sparks fly out in its colours.
  • 9.0 to 9.4: a ring of light spreads out and the board gives a small shake.
  • 9.5 and up: the screen dims, a bigger flame, fireworks in its colours, the score counts up and a banner says its place. It plays the #1 fanfare sound you already have if it goes top, and a tick you can feel.
  • The colours always come from your own colour set, so it fits all twelve without twelve separate shows.
  • Tap anywhere to skip it. Nothing about your data, followers or privacy changes.

Questions for you

  1. Which way: A, B or C?My pick: A. It's the rocket you described and you see it land in its real place. The catch: Save would take you to the Leaderboard instead of the product's page (one tap on the row opens it, and a note at the bottom says "Blue Dream landed at #2 · Open").
  2. One landing for every colour set, in that set's colours, or a different one per set?My pick: one landing in the set's colours. Twelve separate shows is a lot more work and testing for something you see for a few seconds.
  3. Only brand-new products, or also when you move a Log entry up to the Leaderboard?My pick: both, since both put something new on the board. Not when you edit an old one's score.
  4. Reduce Motion on (an iPhone setting): tick it on the right to see. My pick: no flying, the row just fades in with a glow and the 9.5 banner still shows.
