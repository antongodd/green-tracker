Green Tracker · Phase 1 · Design checkpoint

Screen mockups for your approval

Each screen is drawn at iPhone size (390 × 844) using the design brief’s colours, type and spacing. On your iPhone, tap Full screen on any frame to see it at real size. All products, people and prices are made-up examples, and photos are coloured stand-ins. The numbers follow the real rules (Overall, £/g, TOTAL, value for money).

These are the design choices the brief leaves to me. Please approve or change each one. You can reply with the numbers, for example “P2 uppercase, rest fine”.

  1. P1
    Tab bar icons. Four tabs (decided): Leaderboard = podium, Log = notebook, People = two people, More = three dots. Pending requests show as a green count on People.
  2. P2
    Strain tags in Title case (Indica, Sativa, Hybrid) rather than UPPERCASE. Uppercase is already used for the score labels, so title case keeps the two apart.
  3. P3
    Floating + button bottom-right, above the tab bar, on your Leaderboard and Log. Never on someone else’s.
  4. P4
    Sticky controls and sticky country headers. The Rank / Type row stays under the header while you scroll, so an active filter is always visible. Each Log country heading stays pinned while you scroll through its group.
  5. P5
    Editors use a fixed Save bar in place of the tab bar, so Save is always in reach. Cancel sits top-left.
  6. P6
    People layout. A search box on top, then a Requests · Followers · Following switcher. Actions like Remove, Unfollow and Block sit behind a ⋯ button on each row and open a confirmation sheet. Swipe gestures are hidden, so I’ve avoided them.
  7. P7
    Archive: an Un-archive button on each row (same row design as the Leaderboard). No swipe, for the same reason.
  8. P8
    Photo viewer: swipe left and right between a product’s photos, with Close top-left and “Crop this photo” at the bottom.
  9. P9
    Sign-in copy: “Rate, rank and remember everything you’ve tried.” under the title.
Your answers, shown here: a followed person’s rows show Source only (never price). Date tried stays hidden from followers, so rows with no Source have no metadata line at all (see “@mila”). Edible prices stay at 2 decimals (£0.05/mg).
Heads-up for Phase 4: the longest control combination, “Rank: Price per gram” next to “Type: Concentrate”, is tight at 375px (iPhone SE / mini). I’ll measure every combination when the real screen is built and shorten only the ones that don’t fit, as the brief says.
