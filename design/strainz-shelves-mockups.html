Most smoked and Most tried on Strainz

Tap round the phone: a strain on a shelf opens its page, and See all opens the whole list. Every product, name and number here is made up.

Screen
Where the total goes on a strain's page

What changes, as agreed
  • Most smoked: grams from every purchase and Log entry, biggest first, shown as 15.5g. A strain with no weight anywhere isn't on this shelf.
  • Most tried: how many products plus Log entries, shown as ×5.
  • Edibles leave Strainz completely: shelves, See all, strain pages, Compare and a friend's Strainz tab. They stay on the Leaderboard.
  • Log entries are part of Strainz: on a strain's page under "From your Log", no score, so they don't change the average.
  • A strain only in your Log opens a strain page like any other.
  • Friends never see your Log or your grams.
