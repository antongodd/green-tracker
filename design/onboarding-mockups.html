Onboarding for new people

One welcome screen, then a Getting started list that ticks itself off as someone really uses the app, with a hint the first time they reach each screen. Every product, person and seller here is made up.

Tap through it

A new person's first five minutes

The phone works. Go in any order, or follow this:

  1. Let’s go on the welcome screen (or Skip: the list still waits on the Leaderboard).
  2. Tap +, then Save. The first step ticks itself.
  3. Tap Strainz, then Sources. A hint explains each, once.
  4. Tap Feed: the hint points at Friends. Then + to post.
  5. Back on the Leaderboard, tap Pick your Look and choose one.
  6. Tap Done, then your photo (top right) for Show me round again.

B. A slim strip

C. A pill on every screen

Floats above the tab bar wherever you are. Always there, but it covers part of each screen and sits close to the +.

Behind it

What changes, and what doesn’t

  • Who sees it: only accounts created after this goes live. Everyone else gets it only by tapping Show me round again in Settings.
  • The database: one small addition, so the app remembers which steps you’ve done and whether you closed the list, on every phone you sign in on.
  • Export and Restore: restoring a backup doesn’t bring the list back.
  • Privacy and followers: nothing changes. Nobody else sees your progress.
  • Find a friend counts as done when you send an invite or ask to follow someone.
  • Left out: a guided run that locks the screen until you tap Next, and any reminders or notifications.
