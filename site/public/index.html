Private by design

Your stash is your business.

Green Tracker is a journal first. Sharing is something you switch on, person by person.

  • Only friends you approve

    Nobody can follow you, see your Leaderboard or your posts until you say yes.

  • You choose what they see

    Switch off your brands, countries or photos for followers, and mark any product Private. What you paid, where you got it and your notes are never shared.

  • No ads, no tracking

    Nothing is bought or sold here, nothing follows you around the web, and your email is never shown to anyone.

  • Yours to take or delete

    Export everything whenever you like. Delete your account and it’s gone (backups keep a copy for up to 30 days).
